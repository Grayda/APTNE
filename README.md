# APTNE - AusPost Tracking Number Extractor
Extracts a tracking number when you scan in the QR code from an AusPost receipt. Used to make it easier to pass them on to others.

# Usage
Requires Chrome for Android and will not work on iOS due to it using the [Barcode Detection API](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/Barcode_Detection_API). To use this, just go to aptne.grayda.github.io and scan your code. 

# Reasoning
AusPost gives you a receipt when you post something out. If you scan the QR code, it just takes you to the AusPost site, or opens the app. When you scan the barcode on this site, it'll just give you the code, and a short message you can copy to paste into a Messenger conversation or wherever.
